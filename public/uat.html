ROUND 4

The map

Everyone draws on the same map at once. Try to get in each other's way.

Open Map. Drag to move, pinch or use + / − to zoom, then ↺ to reset.

Smooth movement, no stutter or blank patches.

Pick the pencil ✏️ and scribble. Drop a pin 📍 and give it a label.

Your marks show in your colour, and everyone else sees them appear live.

Switch to the eraser 🧽 and remove one of your own marks.

The cursor is a round eraser, not a crosshair. You can rub out your own marks but not other people's.

Go fullscreen with ⤢, then draw and drop a pin while you're in there.

You stay in fullscreen. Getting kicked out was an old bug — shout if it's back.
